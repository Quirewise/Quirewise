# Project-generated fixtures

Synthetic text, data, subtitle, image, vector, archive, ODF, and media samples were generated for Quirewise from original sample content. They are designated CC0. WOFF/EOT fixtures derive from the existing OFL test font; its OFL notice remains applicable. Raw image bytes are RGB8, 64x48 pixels, row-major. DICOM is Explicit VR Little Endian Secondary Capture. Generation notes and staging manifest are kept under `.tmp/sample-stage/generated/` while this workspace is active.
